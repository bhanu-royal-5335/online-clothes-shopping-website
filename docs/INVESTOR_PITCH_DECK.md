# Investor Pitch Deck & Commercial Opportunity

## Rainbow Fashions – Next-Gen Fashion E-Commerce SaaS

---

## Executive Summary Slide
- **The Problem:** Emerging fashion brands struggle with high platform take-rates (up to 30%), high return rates due to bad sizing, and outdated mobile UX.
- **The Solution:** Rainbow Fashions — an enterprise multi-vendor e-commerce SaaS platform featuring **AI Sizing & Outfit Recommendations**, fast checkout, and transparent vendor payouts.

---
